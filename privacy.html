3. Our channels on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook

When you watch, like, comment on or follow our videos, the respective platform processes your data under its own privacy policy: Google/YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook. We only see what the platforms show channel owners: public comments and profile names, and aggregated statistics such as views, watch time and follower counts. We use these statistics to improve our videos (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For Facebook Page Insights, we and Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. are joint controllers; Meta's Page Insights Controller Addendum sets out who is responsible for what.

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply, and delete them when the conversation is finished, unless we are legally required to keep them (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

4. China at Scale Uploader and the YouTube API Services

China at Scale Uploader is a private tool that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It uses the YouTube API Services. Only the owner of the China at Scale channel signs in to it; it has no other users and does not access any other YouTube channel. By using YouTube features through this tool, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

What the tool accesses

After the channel owner signs in with Google (OAuth), the tool may access, using these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload our own finished videos with their title, description and settings.
  • Read channel and video information (youtube.readonly): channel name and ID, and the status of our uploaded videos.
  • Read channel statistics (yt-analytics.readonly): aggregated figures for our own videos, such as views, watch time, likes and subscribers gained. These figures contain no information about individual viewers.

How it uses, stores and shares data

  • Data is used only to publish our own videos and to evaluate how our own videos perform.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's computer, in a file that only the owner's user account can read.
  • Statistics are stored locally as aggregated numbers per video. Data obtained through the YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.
  • Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not combined with data from other sources about individuals.
  • The tool does not use cookies and does not place any files on other people's devices.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working, and we delete the token and all data obtained through the YouTube API Services within 7 days.
